## 🚀 Why WattLoader?

macOS manages power and thermals generically to suit the masses. **WattLoader** is natively built in Swift for engineers, developers, and power-users who demand absolute control over their hardware — whether on **Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4)** or **Intel** Macs.

*   🔋 **Cycle Preservation:** Smart charge thresholds to prevent premature battery degradation during prolonged plugged-in use.
*   🌡️ **Advanced SMC Thermal Control:** Custom fan curves and real-time thermal monitoring to eliminate unwanted *thermal throttling*.
*   🔌 **Deep USB-C Diagnostics:** Power delivery protocol reading, real-time wattage consumption of peripherals, and cable/charger health check.
