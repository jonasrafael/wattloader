## 🧠 Electrochemical Reality: Why 77% Demands Attention

To the average user, **77% health** feels almost full — and when the operating system displays *"Service Recommended"*, it feels like a forced obsolescence trick. 

**Lithium-ion batteries are not a pizza.** Below 80% health, cells accumulate critical internal electrical resistance (impedance). When your processor demands peak power, voltage plunges (cutting execution speed in half), internal temperatures spike, and gas generation threatens your trackpad and internal components.
