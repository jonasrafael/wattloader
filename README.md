## 🧠 The 77% Myth: Why Repair Shops Want You to Panic

To the average user, **77% health** feels almost full — and when your OS pops up with *"Service Recommended"*, it feels like an automated trap to force a purchase. 

**Lithium-ion batteries are not a pizza.** Below 80% health, cells accumulate high internal electrical resistance (impedance). When your processor demands peak power, voltage plunges (slashing your execution speed in half), internal temperatures spike, and gas generation threatens your trackpad and screen.

Shady repair shops love this: they charge a fortune to swap parts you didn't even need to throw away yet.
